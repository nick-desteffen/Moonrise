Throughout history different factions have vied for control within various societies and governments. Each has their own agenda. Now choose which faction you think will help you win on the battlefield in Risk 2210!

This expansion pack includes the official Frontline factions as well as fan-made factions. Each faction page contains additional details.

## Rules

Choose a method for faction selection:

- Each player rolls a die, choose in order
- Each player randomly chooses one card
- Each player randomly chooses two cards, and then keeps one

---

Card designs by [Grinborg](http://boardgamegeek.com/user/burbidge).

[Download Bundled Expansion Pack](https://risk2210.net/expansions/factions_expansion.zip)

The bundled expansion pack contains high-resolution versions of all Faction cards.

This expansion works very well with the Io expansion since it doesn't have any moons. It also works well as a supplemental board for Earth or Mars.

## Rules

When using the Nicky Boy, roll with an 8-sided die:

- 1 or 2 rolled – 1 Ceres
- 3 or 4 rolled – 4 Vesta
- 5 or 6 rolled – 1 Dactyl
- 7 or 8 rolled – 243 Ida

If a player plays the Energy Extraction card, they must possess all 4 asteroids in order to receive the bonus at the end of their turn.

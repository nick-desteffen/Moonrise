## Explanation

Giant Amoebas are invading random locations across the planet! Is it the first step of an alien invasion? Or is this the price humanity must pay for years of dumping toxic waste into the environment? Either way, these creatures are yet another threat you must deal with on your way to global domination!

## Setup

Shuffle and set aside the Invasion of the Giant Amoebas deck, the devastation markers, and the glass counters (which represent the amoebas) at the start of the game.

## Rules

1. At the beginning of a player's turn before they calculate and collect their energy and MODs, that player draws the top card from the Invasion of the Giant Amoebas deck and must follow its instructions. If an invasion card is pulled, the defending player may retreat units from that space to an adjacent space they control before the Giant Amoebas attack.
2. Each Giant Amoeba attacks and defends with a d8. The player to the left of the active player always rolls the attack and defence dice for the Giant Amoebas.
3. A territory taken by Amoebas does not count toward any player's bonuses or score. If a player loses a territory to Amoebas, they do not control that continent.
4. If Giant Amoebas take over a territory that contains a Space Station, that Space Station is destroyed.

## Official FAQs

**What if an Invasion card triggers an attack on the player to my right — who rolls for the Aliens?** You (the player to the left of the player being attacked) roll the d8 for the Aliens.

**Do the Giant Amoebas ever move out of the territory they invaded?** No. They will defend if attacked but they do not invade out of the spaces they control.

**Can I let a nearby opponent retreat through one of my territories?** No.

**Can we use Command Cards against the Giant Amoebas?** No. They are immune to nuclear weapons, and cards such as Stealth MODs cannot be used to defend their invasions.

## Un-Official FAQs

**Do the four devastation markers placed at game start get set aside?** No. The game begins normally with four devastation markers in place. The additional markers from the Amoebas kit supplement those.

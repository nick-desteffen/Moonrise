## Rules

The game begins with both the addition of the Antarctica map and two continents gaining increased continental bonuses: South America (3) and Africa (4). There are additional territory connections from Argentina (South America) to Shackleton (Antarctica), Lesotho (Africa) to Free Afrikaans Republic (Antarctica), and Akara (Indian Ocean) to Independent Kansas (Antarctica). Four additional land territory cards representing the new Antarctica territories are added to the existing land deck and set up normally during the initial phase. One additional Global Warming card is also added into the diplomat deck.

When the Global Warming card is played:

- Destroy all MODs and Commanders in Shackleton, Southern Anarchist Control Zone, and Independent Kansas.
- Place all MODs and Commanders in the Free Afrikaans Republic aside.
- Flip over the Antarctica Board.
- Replace all the MODs and Commanders from the Free Afrikaans Republic back into the Afrikaans Republic on the new map.
- Afrikaans Republic is now part of Africa and must be controlled to claim the Africa bonus.
- High water levels and extreme weather from Global Warming reduce the values of all land continents as indicated on the new Antarctica map.
- If any of the three Antarctica territory cards that are no longer available are turned up while playing another effect, discard that card and draw a new one.
- If an existing card targets one of those territories, that card no longer has any effect.

## Rules

1. There are 4 separate territory card decks: Water, Lunar, Land, and Hydrocarbon.
2. Nuclear command cards affecting water territories allow a player to choose to attack EITHER Water OR Hydrocarbon territories. For example, when playing "Scatter Bomb Water" you may draw random cards from either deck, but must confine your draw to one selected deck.
3. Nuclear command cards affecting lunar territories allow a player to choose BOTH Water AND/OR Lunar territories (since all Water is on Rhea). Players may draw from either or both decks in a nuclear attack.
4. Naval Commander is required to attack to and from water and/or hydrocarbon territories. Once a player reaches the water on the moon, a Naval Commander is the only commander required for attack or movement within those water territories. Naval command cards are playable on either hydrocarbon or water territories.
5. Lunar Commander is required for attack into and out of any lunar colony, but as shown in rule 3, it is not necessary to have a Lunar Commander once underwater. Lunar command cards are playable on either lunar or water territories (Water Commander does not have to be in play).
6. For every 6 unique neutral territories occupied, a player with a Diplomat Commander in play will receive an additional 3 troops at the beginning of each turn.

## Rules

### Setup

Set up the game board, Devastation markers, and initial MOD deployment as usual. Remove the Colony Influence cards from the Command decks (you may replace them with alternatives such as False Intentions for the Diplomat deck). Once all initial MODs are on the board, each player takes the Land Territory card corresponding to the territory where they want their Capital placed. When all players have chosen, reveal them and place a Capital of your color on that territory. Shuffle those cards back into the deck.

### Gameplay

In addition to typical Risk 2210 gameplay:

- Capitals and the territories they occupy are immune to all negative Command Card effects. Frontline Amoebas cannot invade or devastate capitals.
- If your Capital is captured by another player, you are not out of the game.
- Any player with two capitals suffers no detriment for losing one of them.
- No player may build a Space Station in the same territory as a Capital.
- If you control your own Capital, you may hire a Homefront Commander for 3 Energy, played in one of two ways:
  1. The Homefront Commander may not move away from your Capital, but allows all units in that territory to roll 8-sided dice on defense. If you have two Capitals and control both, you may Fortify Move him and other MODs from one to the other as though connected.
  2. The Homefront Commander is as mobile as any other Commander and defends with a d8. If attacking a territory within 2 territories of your Capital, he may roll a d8; otherwise he rolls a d6.
- If a player does not control their own Capital at the start of their turn, they may not collect the 3-Territory Bonus.

### Winning the Game

The game has no 5-turn time limit. Instead, the first player to control a set number of enemy Capitals plus their own wins. That number is: 2 Capitals for 3–4 players; 3 Capitals for 5 players.

## Command Cards

### Homefront Commander

- **Corporate Secrets** — Count: 2, Cost: 0 — After an opponent plays a Frequency Jam card — Ignore the Frequency Jam effect and have the attacking player show their cards to all players.
- **Energy Awareness** — Count: 2, Cost: 0 — Before an invasion is declared — For the rest of the year energy costs are decreased by 1, except for cards that already cost 0 or 1.
- **Infrastructure Improvements** — Count: 2, Cost: 0 — Play at any time — Make a fortification from any territory you control to any other territory, even if not connected. This counts as your fortification move for the next turn.
- **New Atlantis** — Count: 2, Cost: 1 — Play at any time — Move one of your capitals to a water territory of your choice that is under your control.
- **Partizan Alliance** — Count: 2, Cost: 1 — Before your invasion or after an opponent invades your territory — Subtract 1 from your opponent's rolls between the attacking and defending territory for this turn.
- **Patriotic Fervor** — Count: 2, Cost: 1 — Play at any time — Add 4 MODs to a territory that you have a capital on.
- **Seat of Power** — Count: 3, Cost: 1 — Play at any time — Move one of your capitals to a land territory of your choice that is under your control.
- **Space-born Supremacy** — Count: 2, Cost: 1 — Play at any time — Move one of your capitals to a lunar territory of your choice that is under your control.
- **Twin Capitals** — Count: 2, Cost: 2 — Before your first invasion — Place an additional capital on one of your land territories. If you already have two capitals or have no land territories, discard and draw another.
- **Wartime Voting Tax** — Count: 1, Cost: 2 — Before your first invasion — Collect 2 energy and place 2 MODs for each capital you hold.
- **Major Propaganda** — Count: 1, Cost: 4 — Before your first invasion — Roll two d8s. Your opponents must remove that many MODs divided equally between them. They choose the MODs. Add the total MODs to your forces.

### Optional Replacement Cards

- **Boost of Morale (Land)** — Count: 2, Cost: 0 — Before first invasion — This turn, you may change the value of two of your dice to 8 during invasions to or from land territories.
- **False Intentions (Diplomat)** — Count: 4, Cost: 0 — Before first invasion — For the next invasion, the Diplomat Commander can attack with eight-sided dice.
- **Low Gravity (Space)** — Count: 2, Cost: 0 — When an opponent first declares an invasion into a moon territory you occupy — If your opponent wins this invasion, they may not move more units into the territory than the number of dice they last rolled.
- **Sunken Treasures (Water)** — Count: 2, Cost: 0 — After you successfully invade a water territory with your Naval Commander — Collect 2 energy. You may only play 1 copy of this card per turn.
